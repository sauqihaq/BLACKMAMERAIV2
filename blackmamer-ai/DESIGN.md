# BlackMamer AI — Design Direction

## Identity
BlackMamer AI is a private AI workspace with a premium black-and-gold identity. It should feel like a real product made by BlackMamer, not a generic AI dashboard.

## Visual language
- Primary palette: near-black, warm graphite, champagne gold, metallic gold.
- Light mode: warm ivory/cream surfaces with dark brown-black text and the same gold identity.
- Avoid generic purple/blue AI gradients.
- Gold is for identity, focus, actions, active states, and meaningful highlights.
- Use glass only where it improves layering or focus; do not turn every element into glass.

## Workspace
- Sidebar is full-height and remains visually stable while the chat content scrolls.
- Main content has its own vertical scroll area.
- Composer stays available while reading the conversation.
- Agent/model selection lives inside the composer.
- Website artifacts use a preview + source layout.
- Attachments are visible before sending and can be removed.

## Personality
BlackMamer AI speaks Indonesian naturally when the user does. It can use casual "lu/gue" when appropriate, without forcing slang. For emotional conversations it behaves like a supportive friend first; for technical requests it becomes direct and practical.

## Motion
Motion should communicate state: OTP verification, message arrival, typing, attachment changes, artifact preview. Avoid decorative animation with no purpose.
